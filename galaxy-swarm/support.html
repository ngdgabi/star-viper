Quick Controls Reference

  • iPhone & iPad: Left horizontal glide pad to steer; Right FIRE (hold for continuous volley) & NOVA (Hyper-Nova shockwave when bar reaches 100%); top ERA & CHIP pills toggle 1983 C64 / CRT / HD Neon modes live.
  • Mac Keyboard / Mouse: Arrow keys, A/D, or Mouse to steer; Space/Z/Click to fire; X/Shift/Right-Click for Hyper-Nova; V & B keys switch visual & audio eras live.
  • Bluetooth Gamepads: PS5 DualSense, Xbox Wireless Controller, and MFi controllers connect automatically.
