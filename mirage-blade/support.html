Quick Controls Reference

  • iPhone & iPad Touch Controls: Left virtual thumbstick to sprint, crouch, or tiptoe safely over spikes; Right SLASH (drink potion / strike) & LEAP (broad jump / ledge grab / timed parry); top ERA & CHIP buttons switch 1989 EGA & AdLib FM modes live.
  • Mac Keyboard: Arrow keys or WASD to run/climb/crouch; Hold Shift for Careful Sneak & Ledge Catch; Space or Z to Slash / Drink Potion; Up or X to Leap / Parry; V & B toggle 1989 EGA visuals and AdLib FM audio live.
  • Bluetooth Gamepads: PS5 DualSense, Xbox Wireless, and MFi controllers connect automatically with zero setup.
