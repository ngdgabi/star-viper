Quick Controls Reference

  • iPhone & iPad Touch Controls: Left virtual thumbstick to run, crouch, or steer the Hang-Glider; Right CLUB (bonk dinos / mid-air ground-pound) & JUMP buttons; top ERA & CHIP pills toggle 1991 VGA & AdLib FM modes live.
  • Mac Keyboard: Arrow keys or WASD to move; Space or Z for Club Bash (Down + Club in mid-air for Tree-Shaking Ground-Pound!); Up or X to Jump; V & B toggle 1991 VGA visuals and AdLib FM audio live.
  • Bluetooth Gamepads: PS5 DualSense, Xbox Wireless, and MFi gamepads connect automatically.
