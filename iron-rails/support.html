Quick Controls Reference

  • Strategy Map (Mac / iPhone / iPad): Tap or click one of your armies, then tap an adjacent territory to march, merge regiments, or launch a battle. Use RECRUIT to spend Treasury gold and END TURN to advance the campaign.
  • Field Battle, Train Heist & Fort Assault (iPhone & iPad): Left virtual thumbstick to steer, climb ladders, or duck; Right FIRE / PUNCH & UNIT / JUMP buttons; top ERA & CHIP pills switch 1989 C64 / CRT / HD Comic modes live.
  • Mac Keyboard & Gamepads: Arrow keys or WASD to move/climb/duck; Space or Z to Fire/Punch/Jump; Tab or X to switch active unit (Artillery / Cavalry / Infantry); V & B keys switch visual & audio eras live. PS5 DualSense, Xbox, and MFi controllers connect automatically.
