Privacy Policy — Iron Rails: Blue & Gray

Effective Date: November 15, 2026

Iron Rails: Blue & Gray (published by NGD / Gabor Daniel Nagy) respects your privacy completely:

  • Zero Data Collection: The app does not collect, transmit, store externally, or share any personal data, device identifiers, location data, usage analytics, or diagnostics.
  • 100% Offline Local Storage: Your local campaign records, unlocked Commander Medals, and audio/visual preferences are stored solely on your own device via standard Apple UserDefaults.
  • Zero Third-Party SDKs, Ads, or Trackers: The app contains no advertising frameworks, no analytics SDKs, no account creation, and no external network requests.
