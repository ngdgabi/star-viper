IRON RAILS

B L U E & G R A Y

Command continental railroads, blast river bridges with brass artillery, storm timber forts, and hijack speeding bullion steam trains in a lighthearted 120Hz comic-arcade strategy-action game inspired by beloved 1989 8-bit & 16-bit classics.

Universal: iPhone • iPad • Mac Live 1989 C64 Pixel & SID 6581 Mode Zero Ads • Zero IAP

1861–1864 Railway Strategy

Capture 14 territories, secure 4 fortified rail depots for gold train payouts, welcome Atlantic Clipper reinforcements in North Carolina, and dodge roaming storm clouds.

River-Bridge Field Battles

Switch live between Range-Gauge Brass Artillery, Saber Cavalry charges, and Musket Infantry volleys across destructible three-span wooden bridges.

Steam Train Heists & Fort Raids

Sprint across speeding boxcar roofs, leap couplings, duck signal gantries, dodge rolling TNT barrels & guard hounds, and seize the locomotive cab before time runs out!
